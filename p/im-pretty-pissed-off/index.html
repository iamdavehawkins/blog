i'm pretty pissed off

3rd single off the EP landed on streaming platforms this past Friday; I’ll try to keep it short.

The song is about raising hopeful kids in a world where power keeps consolidating into the hands of a tiny, unimaginably wealthy few.

One of those billionaire families successfully lobbied the city of denver and managed to roll back a safe street design - that was planned right in front of my kid’s school - SO THAT THEY COULD TURN OUT OF THEIR GATED NEIGHBORHOOD MORE EASILY.

They hit us high, they hit us low, they hit us right in our backyard. I’m tired of watching them chip away at our future.
